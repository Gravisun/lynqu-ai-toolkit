# §A. Corporate pilot call — Dana Reyes (± Marcus, Priya)

## Objective

One outcome: agreement to scope a 90-day pilot in **one** region. Not a
company-wide rollout, not a security review. If we leave with a named region
and a date, the call worked.

## The positioning lock

Say this, in these words: *"This isn't a CRM replacement. Your CRM is fine at
managing deals — it's blind to the 40% of people your agents meet who never get
entered. We close that gap and hand the clean record to the CRM you already
have."*

## Talk track (15 min)

1. **2 min** — the panel line, back to her
2. **4 min** — the capture gap, with her own event calendar as the example
3. **5 min** — walkthrough: scan → enriched lead → routed → followed up
4. **4 min** — pilot scope: one region, 90 days, one success metric

## Discovery questions

- How does a lead from an open house actually reach the CRM today?
- Who notices when it doesn't?
- What would make this obviously worth expanding past one region?
- Who else has to be comfortable before a pilot starts?

## Anticipated questions

- *"We just renewed our CRM."* — Good. We feed it; we don't replace it.
- *"Our agents won't adopt a new tool."* — It replaces the card they already
  carry. Adoption is the doorway, not a training programme.
- *"How is this different from Popl or Blinq?"* — Those stop at the card. Ours
  is the pipeline after it.

## Landmines

- Do not bring Marcus into a first call. It becomes a security review.
- Do not quote a company-wide price. Priya's threshold is $25k and we are not
  trying to cross it yet.

# §B. Regional call — Elena Kovač

## Objective

Start the 30-day trial on the call. She can; there is no reason to schedule a
second meeting.

## Their world

60 agents, four events a year she runs herself, and a tools budget she doesn't
have to justify upward. Her constraint is her own time, not money.

## Talk track (10 min)

1. **2 min** — her four events, and what happens to the badges after them
2. **5 min** — walkthrough, on her actual next event
3. **3 min** — trial: today, her region, no procurement

## Questions

- What happens to the stack of cards after each event?
- How many of those turn into a conversation two weeks later?
- What would you need to see in 30 days to keep it?

## Landmine

Don't mention the corporate track. If she thinks this needs head-office
approval, she'll wait for it.
